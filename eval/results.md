# Evaluation results

_Generated by `python -m app.eval.run_eval` (`make eval`)._

## Citation verification

- Labeled examples: **48** (24 supports, 24 does-not-support), including hard
  negatives that look similar but say something different.
- Classifier scored with 5-fold cross-validation (never tested on data it trained on).

| Method | Accuracy | Precision | Recall | F1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Baseline (similarity ≥ 0.45) | 0.708 | 0.656 | 0.875 | 0.750 |
| Classifier (logistic regression) | 0.812 | 0.800 | 0.833 | 0.816 |

The classifier wins mainly on **precision** (0.80 vs 0.66): the plain threshold
is fooled by passages that are topically similar but don't actually support the
claim, while the classifier's keyword and numeric-overlap features catch them.

## Cannot-answer (anti-hallucination) check

- Questions the papers cannot answer: **10**
- Correctly refused: **10**
- **False-answer rate: 0.0%**

Every off-topic question fell below the retrieval-similarity floor (0.25) and was
refused before the model was ever asked to draft; the two answerable control
questions were still answered normally.

> Method note: these cannot-answer numbers were produced by running the real
> refusal logic (retrieval-similarity floor → draft → independent verify) with
> `llama3.2:3b` and the local embedding model, against an in-memory corpus in
> place of pgvector (the full Docker stack wasn't spun up in this pass). Re-run
> the exact production path against a user's uploaded papers with
> `python -m app.eval.run_eval --live --user-id <uuid>`.
